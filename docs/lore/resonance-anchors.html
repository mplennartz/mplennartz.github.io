RESONANCE ANCHORS

📖 OVERVIEW

"Resonance Anchor" is Investigator 89's term for the fixed points the displaced team must reach to leave each era. The chroniton detector tracks an anchor's artificial signal. Pressing the detector's contact plate against the anchor tears open a temporary passage into another era. According to Investigator 89, there is no other way out.

✅ KNOWN FACTS

CONFIRMED:
- Anchor 1 (Late Cretaceous): a fossilized dinosaur skull in a tar-pit swamp. Contact caused a violent energy surge and a rift that showed the next era (desert and pyramids) before it opened.
- Anchor 2 (Middle Kingdom Egypt): presumed reached; the packet covering it (TX-225-005) was lost.
- Anchor 3 (Imperial Rome): located somewhere inside the Palatine palace precinct. Not yet reached.
- Approaching an anchor, the detector goes from pulsing to a steady hum, and its aperture extends fully.
- The detector's signal weakened during the Egypt displacement. In Rome the team estimated a 72-hour window.

❓ OPEN QUESTIONS

UNRESOLVED:
- Who placed the anchors, and whether they were placed for the team
- Why the first anchor was already a fossil in an era when its species may still have been alive
- Whether each era is real history or, as Investigator 89 suggests, a "simulation"
- Whether any chain of anchors leads back to 1999
- What happens to the team if an era's window closes before they reach its anchor
