📡 PACKET TX-225-001 — FIRST LIGHT

Local: ~66,000,000 BC | Signal Integrity: 41%

The packet begins mid-recording. The last coherent facility data puts the team inside the NeXstep reactor control suite: fail-safes deactivated, the core chamber open and pulsing, and Investigator 89 at a terminal trying to shut the system down. Then a bright flash, and ▓▓▒░ NO DATA ░▒▓▓.

When the signal resumes, the team is coming to in a forest. The audio track has birdsong that isn't birdsong. The air is described as wetter, the woods as "off." A handheld device, matching the one seen on the reactor-suite desk, is lying at Investigator 89's feet. Within seconds the recording picks up a low growl and a ground-borne rumble. The last legible field annotation reads: "T-REX."

📡 PACKET TX-225-002 — CHRONITON

Local: ~66,000,000 BC | Signal Integrity: 57%

The team outruns the tyrannosaur. Once clear, they notice Investigator 66 is carrying a device that has begun to pulse. Investigator 28 identifies it as a chroniton detector. Chronitons are subatomic particles with temporal properties, and detectors like this are used to track time travel and temporal anomalies. The detector points toward a strong source, and the team follows it.

The trail leads into a cave and a vast chamber: a sheer drop, a narrow ledge running west, and two Pteranodons circling overhead. Partway along the ledge, one of them seizes Investigator 89. The two animals fight over him in midair until he is dropped roughly 100 feet into a fast river below. The rest of the team gets out and finds him washed ashore downstream.

Investigator 89 examines the detector. Its pulses repeat at fixed intervals that don't match magnetic, geothermal or seismic activity, so the source is stable and artificial. Near stronger readings it adds a second, layered tone and its internal parts vibrate in time with the source. In Investigator 89's view, it is trying to synchronize with whatever is transmitting. He connects his laptop and reprograms the detector into a directional compass. When it is pointed at the source, an aperture slides open and shows a contact plate. The plate isn't made for a fingerprint; it looks made to connect to another device.

At the edge of a swamp the aperture is almost fully open. The ground is covered in overlapping tracks; Investigator 07 reads it as a hunting ground. The team cuts makeshift spears from branches. Investigator 28 spots a large white object far out in the marsh and plots a route to it. The closer they get, the harder the detector works, as if it wants to be joined to the object.

The route passes through a dark grove of tall trees and tar pools. Near a strange glow, three Velociraptors attack. When the packet cuts off, one raptor is down and one is wounded ░▒▓ SIGNAL LOST ▓▒░

📡 PACKET TX-225-003 — THE WHITE SKULL

Local: ~66,000,000 BC | Signal Integrity: 72%

Four more raptors arrive from the west. The team kills five of the six; the last one runs. They cross the tar on a bridge of old bones and reach the white object: a fossilized dinosaur skull.

The detector stops pulsing and settles into a steady hum. The aperture is fully open, with the contact plate sticking out about an inch. Investigator 89 presses the detector against the skull. A violent surge of energy runs through the marsh and the air tears open into a shimmering rift. Through it the team sees a vast desert under a blazing sun, with pyramids rising in the distance. The opening starts to shake, and then there is a flash.

🗂️ BUREAU ANALYST NOTES

Packet TX-225-001 has the device at Investigator 89's feet; by TX-225-002, Investigator 66 is carrying it. Analysts cannot yet confirm whether the "tricorder-class" unit from the reactor suite and the chroniton detector are the same device.

The headcount in TX-225-001 confirms six investigators. A seventh, partly legible field reads "5_"; it may refer to Investigator 51, who was present at NeXstep. Status unconfirmed.

The skull is the first recorded Resonance Anchor. Note that it was already a fossil in an era when its species may still have been alive.
