A Cup of Friendship

LOCAL DATE: 29 December 192 CE (dated by Investigator 28)

SOURCE: Bureau Field Telemetry Unit (Class T) / chroniton detector

📡 PACKET TX-225-006 — PALATINE

Local: 29–30 December 192 CE | Signal Integrity: 58%

The team arrives near the Forum. Guarded palace buildings rise along the Palatine above them. The detector gives one weak pulse toward the imperial precinct and goes quiet. The team estimates it has about 72 hours to reach the anchor. From posters and coins, Investigator 28 dates the moment to 29 December 192 CE, with Commodus as emperor. Investigator 89 knows how it ends: Commodus is assassinated on 31 December after exercising and bathing, and the Praetorian prefect Aemilius Laetus is part of the plot.

Investigator 13 steals tunics for the team. Investigator 89 then makes a food vendor see a handful of rocks as denarii, which buys about five days of provisions. A bluff to the Praetorian guard, claiming to be entertainers sent for by Laetus, fails. The team gets onto the Palatine by stealth instead. The detector points toward the imperial baths. A guard patrol spots them, and they lose it after a short chase.

Inside the baths, Investigator 28 picks out Laetus himself. Investigator 66 notices a man being oiled and tended by several servants. He walks straight over, and Investigator 89 overhears enough to identify him as Narcissus, the gladiator trainer Laetus recruited to kill the emperor. Narcissus has the team strip and run through exercises; he is sizing them up for tomorrow's games. Investigator 89 says Laetus sent them. Narcissus asks for a writ, decides Laetus must have forgotten to give them one, and writes his own pass so they can move freely on the Palatine. A detector reading shows the anchor is not in the baths but somewhere in the palace.

Narcissus is still suspicious, so Investigator 89 claims the team met Laetus on campaign. In front of Laetus, the prefect asks whether Narcissus has brought them into the plot against Commodus. Investigator 89, the team's only Latin speaker, says yes. Laetus is skeptical but writes a writ that grants access to the palace itself.

First, though, they have to pass Narcissus's dawn trial. The team sleeps at his gladiator camp. Some drill with arena weapons, some scout the Colosseum, and some rest. In the morning they walk into the arena. The packet ends ▓▒░ MID-ENGAGEMENT ░▒▓.

🗂️ BUREAU ANALYST NOTES

The team is now inside the conspiracy to assassinate a Roman emperor, holding writs from both Laetus and Narcissus. The recorded date of the assassination is 31 December 192 CE, so the team's 72-hour window ends just as the plot is carried out.

Investigator 89's ability to make a merchant see rocks as coins is consistent with an anomalous perception effect. That is logged for review and is not part of Investigator 89's documented skills.

⚠️ HISTORICAL INTERFERENCE RISK ⚠️

The team is directly involved in a recorded historical event. The Bureau cannot predict the consequences of any deviation. Monitor for further packets around the clock.
