🗂️ BUREAU ANALYST NOTES

This is the first contact of any kind since the NeXstep Displacement Event. How the Class T unit reached Bureau receivers is not understood. No relay path exists, and the packet header carries a timestamp our systems cannot parse. The fauna described, if accurate, puts the team tens of millions of years in the past. The Bureau is treating it as temporal displacement until something disproves it.

Headcount appears consistent with the deployed team plus the guest civilian on site. The unit appears to be transmitting on its own or at regular intervals; it is unclear whether the team knows it is doing so.
