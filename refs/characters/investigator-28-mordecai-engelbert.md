## Background Dossier
Born in New Haven, Connecticut, Mordecai was then primarily raised in Cusco, Peru by American expatriates who moved there to study the Incan culture and ruins, teaching Incan studies at the nearby UNSAAC university (Universidad Nacional de San Antonio Abad del Cusco).

It was there, high in the Andes mountains near the slopes of the majestic Machu Picchu, that Mordecai first discovered his love for exploring and researching the unknown. After finishing his master's degree in the States on the Superstitions and Supernatural Beliefs of Ancient Cultures, he quickly crossed paths with Ezra Cason due to similar academic circles and interests. After doing some independent consulting work for some wealthy private clients as an overqualified treasure hunter, Mordecai eventually found himself an arguably more moral (if not less safe) job working for an obscure branch of the FBI of all places...

His Explorer background and fondness for thrown weapons and field chemistry make him as useful clearing a path into a site as he is interpreting what is found there.
